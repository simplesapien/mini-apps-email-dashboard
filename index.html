How to read these numbers

Only product user IDs mapped to confirmed Klaviyo recipients are counted. Post-send activity is associated with the send, but does not prove the email caused it. Routine use or another nudge may be responsible. Email cards are cumulative through one cutoff, overlap, and must not be added together. Klaviyo-confirmed human clicks are shown separately.
