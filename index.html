How to read these numbers

Only the product user IDs belonging to actual email recipients are counted after the send. Post-send activity is a strong signal, but it does not prove the email caused the visit: routine use, an earlier email, a product notification, a bookmark, or a workplace message could also bring someone back. Klaviyo-confirmed human clicks are shown separately.
